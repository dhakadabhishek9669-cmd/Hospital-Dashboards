How to Use the Dashboards

Each dashboard has the same layout and controls — here's how to get the most out of them

Step 01
🎛️
Select Your Filters
Choose a specific Bay or Area, set the bill time (5 / 7 / 10 min), and adjust peak and surplus thresholds. All 6 sections update instantly.
Step 02
📊
Read the Heatmap
Red cells = over capacity. Green = surplus. Look for red hours — those are your rebalancing opportunities. Biller count shown in each cell.
Step 03
💡
Act on Recommendations
Scroll to the bottom recommendations section. It auto-matches deficit hours with surplus areas and tells you exactly where to redeploy billers.
Dashboard Features
⚡
Shift-Aware Capacity
Capacity recalculates by hour based on actual biller shift start and end times — not just headcount.
🔄
Live Bill Time Toggle
Switch between 5, 7 and 10 min per bill. Every chart and cell recalculates instantly without reload.
📈
6 Visualisation Sections
Grouped bar chart, trend lines, utilisation %, bar meters, heatmap, and auto-recommendations.
🎯
Peak Hour Detection
Set your own threshold (80% / 100% / 120%). Peak hours highlighted in red across all charts and the heatmap.
🗺️
Manpower Gap Heatmap
Area × Hour matrix showing utilisation %, billers on duty, and exact over/under capacity per cell.
💬
Auto Recommendations
System auto-generates rebalancing suggestions — deficit areas matched with surplus areas at the same hour.
